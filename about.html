About Next Level Gaming

Founded in 2023 by Jason Naidoo, based in Johannesburg, South Africa.

Our Mission

To provide a world-class gaming experience that brings together casual players and competitive esports enthusiasts in a modern, welcoming environment.

Our Vision

To become the leading esports hub in South Africa, nurturing local talent and hosting international-standard tournaments.

Our Facilities

50 high-end PC stations, 10 console lounges, a dedicated streaming booth, and a cafeteria for refreshments.
