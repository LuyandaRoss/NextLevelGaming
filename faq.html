Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

📅 Booking & Pricing

Do I need to book in advance?
Yes, we highly recommend booking online through our Calculate Fees page to guarantee your station, especially on weekends.


Can I cancel my booking?
Cancellations made 24 hours before the booking time are fully refundable. Later cancellations incur a 50% fee.


Do you offer group discounts?
Yes! Groups of 5 or more receive a 10% discount automatically applied at checkout.

🎮 Arena Rules & Facilities

Is there an age limit?
We welcome all ages! However, children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult.


Can we bring our own food?
Outside food is not permitted, but we have a fully stocked snack bar and cafeteria on-site.


Do you have parking?
Yes, secure underground parking is available free of charge for all customers.

🏆 Tournaments & Events

How do I register for a tournament?
Create an account, navigate to the Tournaments page, and click "Register Team" for the event you wish to enter.


What is the prize pool?
Prize pools vary by event and are listed on the Tournaments page. Payouts are made via EFT within 48 hours.


Can I host a private event?
Absolutely! Contact us via the Contact page for corporate and birthday party bookings.

💻 Technical & Hardware

What are the PC specs?
All PCs feature NVIDIA RTX 4080 GPUs, Intel i9 processors, 32GB RAM, and 240Hz monitors.


Can I bring my own mouse and keyboard?
Yes, you are welcome to bring your own peripherals. We provide sanitized wipes for your gear.


Do you have Wi-Fi?
Yes, we have high-speed fiber Wi-Fi available for all customers free of charge.
